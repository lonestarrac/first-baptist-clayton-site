Membership

What it means to be a member at First Baptist Church of Clayton.

Welcome to First Baptist Church of Clayton! We're thrilled you're exploring what it means to join our church family. Becoming a member here is more than signing up — it's a joyful commitment to walk together in faith, grow in Christ, and serve our community. Rooted in Scripture and guided by our love for Jesus, we invite you to discover the heart of membership and the expectations that shape our shared journey.

A Heart for Jesus and His Church

At First Baptist, membership starts with a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. We believe that faith in Him as Savior and Lord transforms lives (Romans 10:9–10). If you've trusted Jesus and been baptized by immersion as a believer — either here or at a church with similar beliefs — you're already on the path to joining us! If you haven't been baptized by immersion, our elders are happy to talk with you about this meaningful step of obedience.

Membership is a covenant, a promise to live out your faith alongside others. It's about saying, “I'm all in for Jesus and His mission through this church!” We're a family, and we'd love for you to be part of it.

What We Believe and Promise Together

As a member, you'll embrace our Statement of Faith, which anchors us in core biblical truths — like the authority of Scripture, salvation by grace through faith, and the call to love God and others. You'll also commit to our Church Covenant, a pledge to pursue holiness, unity, and service together. Don't worry if this sounds big — we'll walk you through it in our membership class, where you'll learn about our beliefs, mission, and vision to transform our community for Christ.

The Membership Journey

Joining First Baptist is simple but meaningful, designed to ensure you're ready to thrive in our family:

  • Share Your Story — Meet with our pastor or one of our elders to share your testimony of faith. We love hearing how God has worked in your life!
  • Learn with Us — Attend our membership class to explore our beliefs, covenant, and expectations. It's a chance to ask questions and connect.
  • Join the Family — After elder approval, our church affirms new members in a business meeting with a joyful vote. Then, we celebrate you in a worship service!

Living as a Member: Our Shared Expectations

Being a member means actively participating in our church's life, rooted in love and commitment. Here's what that looks like:

  • Worship Together — Join us regularly for Sunday worship (at least monthly, unless providentially hindered). It's where we grow through preaching, singing, and fellowship.
  • Give Generously — Support our mission through tithes or offerings, as God leads you. Every gift helps us share the Gospel!
  • Serve Joyfully — Use your gifts in ministry — whether teaching kids, greeting guests, or serving our community. There's a place for you!
  • Stay Accountable — Live out biblical values and submit to loving church discipline if needed. We're here to help each other grow (Galatians 6:1).
  • Pray and Engage — Pray for our church, leaders, and mission. Stay connected through Bible studies, Wednesday programs, or outreach.

If life gets tough (illness, travel, etc.), let us know — we'll walk with you to keep you connected. After all, we're a family that cares deeply!

Why Membership Matters

Membership isn't just a formality; it's a commitment to belong, grow, and serve. It's saying, “I'm here to follow Jesus with you, to celebrate the highs and support through the lows.” Whether you're new to faith or transferring from another church, we're excited to welcome you into a community that loves God and loves each other.

Ready to take the next step?

If you're ready to explore membership, reach out to our Lead Pastor, Wesley Morgan, or any elder. We'll guide you to the next membership class and answer any questions. You can also email us at fbcclayton309@gmail.com or stop by the welcome desk on Sunday. We can't wait to see how God uses you in our family!
